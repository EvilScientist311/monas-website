<p class="mona-lead">A connected approach to caring for elderly parents living independently in the Tricity.</p>

A special focus of Dr. Mona Chopra’s practice is supporting families living outside India whose elderly parents live independently in the Tricity.

## Regular assessment

Regular health and functional assessments can help families understand their parents’ medical needs, mobility, daily function and overall wellbeing.

## Coordinated medical care

When required, Dr. Chopra can coordinate with hospitals and specialists, helping bring together the healthcare support around the older adult.

## Guidance for caregivers

Practical guidance can support caregivers with day-to-day care, medication concerns, nutrition, mobility and maintaining independence.

## Keeping families informed

Dr. Chopra can keep families informed about their parents’ health and overall wellbeing, supporting communication and shared understanding across distance.

<div class="mona-callout"><h2>Care centred on the older adult</h2><p>The focus remains on health, functional ability, quality of life and dignity, alongside support for the family and caregivers.</p></div>
