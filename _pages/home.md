## My approach

Ageing is not simply about managing diseases. It is about maintaining the ability to move, think, function, connect and live independently.

I work with older adults and their families to identify health and functional concerns early, coordinate appropriate medical care, and create practical plans for healthier ageing at home.

## Who can benefit?

- Adults 50+ who want to age proactively
- Older adults living with multiple health concerns
- Families caring for ageing parents
- Older adults who prefer assessment and care at home
- People experiencing reduced mobility, strength, memory or independence

## A coordinated approach

Where appropriate, care can be coordinated with physiotherapists, nutrition professionals, psychologists, occupational therapists, specialists and other healthcare providers to create a more complete support system around the older adult.
