A comprehensive approach to the health, function and wellbeing of older adults.

## Comprehensive healthy-ageing assessment

- Medical and medication review
- Functional and mobility assessment
- Falls-risk assessment
- Nutrition and muscle-health review
- Sleep and lifestyle assessment
- Cognitive and emotional wellbeing screening
- Preventive health review
- Personalised healthy-ageing plan

## Geriatric home visits

For older adults who find it difficult or inconvenient to travel for consultation.

Assessment and guidance may include:

- Multiple health conditions
- Medication concerns
- Reduced mobility or recurrent falls
- Frailty and loss of strength
- Memory or cognitive concerns
- Nutrition and weight changes
- Functional decline
- Coordination with specialists and allied healthcare professionals

## Caregiver & family sessions

Practical guidance for families caring for an ageing parent, including:

- Understanding changes in ageing
- Medication and care coordination
- Falls and home safety
- Nutrition and hydration
- Cognitive and behavioural concerns
- Maintaining independence
- Caregiver stress and family communication
- Knowing when further medical assessment is needed

## Healthy ageing & preventive health

For adults who want to proactively plan the next phase of life—with a focus on strength, mobility, brain health, nutrition, sleep, meaningful activity and independence.
