<p class="mona-eyebrow">MBBS, MD (GERIATRICS)</p>
<p class="mona-lead">Geriatrician | Healthy Ageing & Elder Care</p>

Dr. Mona Chopra is a trained Geriatrician with extensive clinical experience in hospital medicine, geriatrics, rehabilitation and emergency care. Her approach focuses on comprehensive assessment and management of older adults, with emphasis on functional ability, mobility, cognition, medication review, chronic disease management, nutrition and quality of life.

## Areas of geriatric care

- Comprehensive Geriatric Assessment
- Falls, Frailty & Mobility
- ADL / IADL & Functional Assessment
- Cognitive & Medication Assessment
- Chronic Disease & Preventive Care
- Nutrition & Lifestyle
- Post-hospitalisation & Rehabilitation
- Caregiver & Family Guidance

## Education

**MD / Master’s in Geriatrics** — University of New South Wales, Australia

**MBBS** — JSS Medical College, Mysore

## Professional membership

Life Associate Member — Geriatric Society of India

## Clinical experience

Experience across Geriatrics, Rehabilitation, Emergency Medicine, Paediatrics and Hospital Medicine, including management of medical emergencies and multidisciplinary patient care.
